Course and project work in robot motion planning, with emphasis on the relationship between **search, geometry, optimization, and executable trajectories**.

The work includes implementing and evaluating planning methods, reasoning about collision-free motion, and studying how trajectory quality changes under different planning and optimization choices.

### Topics

- graph and sampling-based planning
- collision checking and configuration spaces
- trajectory optimization
- planning performance and path quality
