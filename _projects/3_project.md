Project work on autonomous mobile robots, connecting **perception and state estimation** with **planning and navigation**.

The work studies how an agent builds an internal estimate of the world and then uses that estimate to choose actions under partial observability and uncertainty.

### Topics

- localization and state estimation
- mapping and SLAM
- graph-based and sampling-based planning
- autonomous exploration and navigation
