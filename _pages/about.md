I am an MEng student in Mechanical & Industrial Engineering at the **University of Toronto**, working on robotics and autonomous systems.

My current research focuses on **structured representations for complex robot planning**: how a robot can represent objects, states, actions, and reusable procedures in a form that supports reliable long-horizon execution. I am particularly interested in **3D scene graphs**, **procedural memory**, and the interface between symbolic structure and learned robot policies.

More broadly, my interests include robot planning and control, reinforcement and imitation learning, multimodal robot learning, SLAM, and state estimation.

## Current work

- **3D scene graphs for complex task planning** — building a structured task/world representation and reusable procedural memory for embodied agents.
- **Robot motion planning** — studying planning under geometric and dynamical constraints, including sampling-based planning and trajectory optimization.
- **Mobile robotics and perception** — navigation, mapping, state estimation, and autonomous decision-making in partially observed environments.
