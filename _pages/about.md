I am an MEng student at the University of Toronto working in robotics. My research interests are in **robot planning, structured world models, robot learning, and 3D scene understanding**, with a focus on how robots can reason about and execute long-horizon tasks.

My current research explores **3D scene graphs and reusable procedural memory for complex task planning**. I am studying how a robot can represent objects, states, actions, constraints, and reusable task procedures in a structured world model; retrieve the right procedure from an instruction and scene context; and execute it robustly in simulation.

I am also interested in **task and motion planning, planning and control, SLAM and state estimation, autonomous navigation, imitation learning, and reinforcement learning**.

You can find my public work on [GitHub](https://github.com/lazur07) and on the [projects](/projects/) page.
